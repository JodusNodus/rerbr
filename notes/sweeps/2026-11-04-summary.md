## Fixed

- **Physics:** nothing to fix. 324 runs had no declined, unbound or NaN step and no panic. The 56 that did not finish
  were autopilot, damage or stage-geometry cases.
- **Load:** 6 stages panicked at load and now start.
  - SS Ai-Petri and Uchan-Su, 4 variants: their garbage geometry near `f32::MAX` also drew the stage as night under
    a white haze, and they now look like their neighbours.
  - Inland Road II.
  - Aguimes Santa Lucia 2025.
- All 78 BTB stages loaded and drew.

Checks: `cargo test -p rbr-render` passes, and `scripts/rust-check.sh` fmt, clippy and input_check are ok. The
launcher has two test failures: they need `RBR_NGP_DLL`, which this environment lacks, and they fail on main too.
Force feedback and sound were off for every run.

1. **Stages that borrow textures from an encrypted sibling** draw magenta: Erken II, Ruapuke, Whaanga Coast Sprint,
   Inland Road II (almost all magenta). List them as unavailable, like their siblings?
2. **Scrambled `TextureFilenameMap<id>.ini`**: Ponteareas 2008, Savopohja, Narva and El Fargue, all dated
   2026-10-25 like the encrypted stages' remap files. It looks like RallySimFans's encryption, so ReRBR does not
   decode them, and 4 to 17 textures per stage draw magenta. Fosso delle Dogane's file is scrambled too, but none of
   its textures miss. Draw them as they are, or list them as unavailable?
3. **Missing textures:** keep the magenta stand-in, or draw a missing texture untextured, as the game most likely
   does?
4. **BTB 1054 and 1077:** the spawn is 49 m and 21 m off the driveline. Is that the authors' data as intended?
   Should the autopilot's off-road rule ignore the countdown?
5. **Open, not examined:**

   - Verkiai reverse with the i20: the autopilot neither recovers nor gives up.
   - 51 stages name spectator Lua scripts that exist in neither the install nor the retail archive. The 1.02
